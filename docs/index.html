For toddlers & preschoolers

Tap a toy. Hear a story. That's the whole app.

TapStory turns an old iPhone into a screen-free storytelling box. The screen locks itself and ignores touches -- the only thing that does anything is tapping a toy or card against the phone. Tap a stuffed monkey, hear a story about a magic monkey. Tap a letter card, hear that letter and a matching word.

How it works

Set up once, then hand the phone to your toddler with confidence.

1

Stick a tag on a toy

A cheap NFC sticker on the bottom of any toy or a laminated card -- no special hardware.

2

Record or type a story

Type what should be said (spoken on-device, no recording needed) or use your own voice.

3

Turn on Guided Access

iOS's built-in Guided Access locks the phone to just this app -- no Home button, no App Switcher.

4

Hand it over

Your child taps the toy against the phone. That's the entire interaction model.

What your child sees

Calm, static, and quiet by default -- the interesting thing happens on the toy, not the screen.

Why it's built this way

Every design choice traces back to one idea: the phone should feel like an appliance, not a screen.

🔒

Actually screen-free

The app locks itself and ignores every touch except an invisible parent gate. Pair it with iOS Guided Access and there's nothing else on the phone to reach.

🎤

Type it or say it

Type a sentence and TapStory speaks it on-device -- no recording required. Or use your own voice for a more personal touch.

🏷️

Any toy, any card

A couple of dollars of NFC stickers turn any stuffed animal, book, or flashcard into a "magic" one, matched to a story you choose.

👤

Built for parents

A hidden parent gate keeps settings, recording, and tag-writing out of reach of curious little hands.

🔒

No accounts, ever

Nothing to sign up for, nothing syncing to a server. Everything you create stays on that phone.

📈

Grows with your kid

Stories today, alphabet and vocabulary cards tomorrow -- add new tags any time, at your own pace.

No data collection

TapStory doesn't have an internet connection to talk to. No analytics, no ads, no tracking -- not even anonymized.

Everything stays on-device

Recordings, stories, and your tag library are stored only in this phone's local storage. Nothing is ever uploaded.

Built for kids' privacy

No accounts means no personal information is ever collected from a child, intentionally or otherwise.
